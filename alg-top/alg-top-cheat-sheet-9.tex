State the relative version of Mayer-Vietoris and the LES of triples 

LES of triples 
Given that \( A \subseteq B \subseteq C \) and it exists the SES 
\( 0 \longrightarrow C_n(B, A) \longrightarrow C_n(C, A) \longrightarrow C_n(C, B) \longrightarrow 0\).

there is a LES:
\( \dots \longrightarrow H_n(B, A) \longrightarrow H_n(C, A) \longrightarrow H_n(C, B) \longrightarrow H_{n-1}(B, A) \longrightarrow \dots \).

The relative version of Mayer-Vietoris states for \( Y \subseteq X \) and \( Y = \text{int}(A) \cup \text{int}(B) \)
with \( A \subseteq U,\ B \subseteq V \) the usual decomposition of the space \( X \) for Mayer-Vietoris, it holds

\( \dots \longrightarrow H_n(U \cap V, A \cap B) \longrightarrow H_n(U, A) \oplus H_n(V, B) \longrightarrow H_{n}(X, Y) \longrightarrow \dots \).
